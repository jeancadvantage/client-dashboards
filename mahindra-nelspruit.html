Cost per lead, three-month trend

Combined spend held roughly steady, but Google Ads delivery collapsed in August (impressions -72.9%, clicks -73.1% month-on-month), so the blended cost per lead climbed sharply from R22,16 in June to R62,64 in August — worth investigating what changed with the Google Ads account.

What's likely driving August's spike

On 13 July, WhatsApp button clicks — which made up ~97% of June's tracked conversions (352 of 364) — were demoted from a primary to a secondary conversion action in Google Ads. If the account is running Smart Bidding (Target CPA / Maximize Conversions), that algorithm optimizes toward whatever it's told counts as a conversion. Losing its dominant signal overnight would explain the pattern below almost exactly: ad quality held steady, but Google pulled back delivery and paid more per click while it tried to recalibrate against a much thinner conversion signal.
