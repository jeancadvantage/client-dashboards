What's likely driving August's spike

On 13 July, WhatsApp button clicks — which made up ~97% of June's tracked conversions (352 of 364) — were demoted from a primary to a secondary conversion action in Google Ads. If the account is running Smart Bidding (Target CPA / Maximize Conversions), that algorithm optimizes toward whatever it's told counts as a conversion. Losing its dominant signal overnight would explain the pattern below almost exactly: ad quality held steady, but Google pulled back delivery and paid more per click while it tried to recalibrate against a much thinner conversion signal.

Ad relevance (CTR) barely moved — this looks like a bidding/delivery problem following the tracking change, not a drop in ad quality or audience interest. This is the most likely explanation based on the report data; confirming it would need a look inside the account at bid strategy and budget settings for July–August.

Leads: Google Ads changed its conversion tracking on 13 July — WhatsApp button clicks moved from a primary to a secondary conversion action, which would make the platform's raw monthly "Conversions" figures misleading if compared directly (364 → 158 → 20). To keep months comparable, "Leads" here is the total tracked conversions (primary + secondary, i.e. form submissions + WhatsApp clicks) for each month: June 364, July ≈257, August 125.

Spend: neither the Google Ads nor Social Ads reports state total monthly spend directly for Mahindra. Both are estimated — Google Ads as avg. CPC × clicks, Social as avg. cost-per-landing-page-view × landing page views — and flagged accordingly throughout.

Conversion rate: the "How this compares" widget uses a blended conversion rate (total leads ÷ Google Ads clicks) rather than Google's own reported conversion rate, since Google's figure undercounts WhatsApp conversions after the 13 July tracking change. The Google Ads channel card below still shows Google's own reported rate separately, labeled as such — the two numbers are calculated differently and won't match.

Data note: the July report's per-creative social ad breakdown table appears to duplicate June's exact figures (a likely rendering issue in that report), so it wasn't used here — July's social totals above come from that report's own top-line stats and narrative text, both of which cross-check correctly against August's month-over-month comparisons.

Lead type breakdown: not included for Mahindra — its campaign structure (vehicle models + OJA tractors) doesn't map cleanly onto a single consistent category split the way Subaru's Used/New/Service campaigns did.
